The bottom line

The collision is one band wide. The clear air is the rest of the page.

Besty and Virn genuinely overlap on only three things — the SOP builder, the AI knowledge base, and checklists — and even there, Besty does them STR-flavored and STR-locked. Their entire middle section (guest messaging, upselling, OTA inbox) is a vertical you've chosen not to chase.

Your structural advantage is the bottom band: a configurable engine and flexible data model that serve commercial, retail, industrial, residential, LTR — and IT Ops. Besty's speed comes precisely from being narrow; that same narrowness is what stops them from ever following you out of short-term rentals.

So don't clone Besty's builder — clone its feel ("describe it, watch it build") on top of an engine that's vertical-agnostic underneath. That's the whole game.
