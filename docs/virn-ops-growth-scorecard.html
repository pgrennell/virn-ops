What this means for Virn Ops

The static SOP/checklist tier is being commoditized from above.

Every dollar and every headline is flowing to two places: AI-native orchestration (n8n, Gumloop, Lindy) and AI-powered capture + "what should we automate" intelligence (Scribe). Your four named comps sit in the slow lane — Process Street is repositioning to compliance to survive, and Tallyfy / SweetProcess / Manifestly are stable small businesses the category has simply grown past.

The sharpest signal is Scribe Optimize: capture the work, then tell the customer where AI/agents should take over. That's the exact bridge between your KB + SOP layer and the automation layer — and it's the highest-value real estate on this board.

Implication: don't build "a better checklist." Build the layer that turns a captured SOP into something a human runs, an AI assists, or an agent executes — and win a vertical (property ops) before the horizontal giants notice.
