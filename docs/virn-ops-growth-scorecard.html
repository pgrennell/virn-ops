What the updated board tells you

Money flows to scale players. Mindshare in your lane flows to verticals.

The top of the board hasn't changed: every dollar and headline is still going to AI-native orchestration (n8n, Gumloop, Lindy) and AI-powered capture + automation intelligence (Scribe). Your four named comps still sit in the slow lane.

The new fifth band is the one to watch carefully. Besty is the only product on the board that has actually shipped your Virn Ops concept — a Workflow & SOP Builder plus an AI Knowledge Base — and they did it by locking themselves into one vertical (STR) to move fast. Propvana is doing a far narrower version of the same play (voice AI, two workflows, LTR-residential). Both are small in absolute funding terms but they're the closest live analogues to what you're building.

The strategic read: you don't beat them on speed inside their own verticals — you beat them by being the configurable, cross-vertical platform underneath that they can't become without rebuilding from scratch. They validate the thesis; their narrowness validates your wedge.
